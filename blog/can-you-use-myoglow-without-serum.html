Can You Use MyoGlow Without Serum? What Works Best

By Sarah Kensington • May 31, 2026 • 5 min read

Yes, you can use MyoGlow without a serum. However, pairing it with a conductive, hydrating serum improves glide, enhances thermal and light penetration, and protects fragile skin. For best results, a serum is strongly recommended.

Key Takeaways

  • MyoGlow functions without serum, but friction can irritate crepey skin and reduce treatment comfort.
  • A lightweight serum supports smoother movement and may boost the absorption of therapeutic light and heat.
  • Official No-Tox serum is formulated for MyoGlow, but any water-based, conductive serum without heavy oils can work.
  • Skipping serum won’t damage the device, but it may slow visible results and make sessions less pleasant.
  • For maximum firming and texture improvement, a daily 5-minute session with a quality serum is the most effective approach.

How Serum Affects MyoGlow Performance

The MyoGlow device uses red light therapy, micro-sculpting pulses, gentle warmth, and sonic massage. A serum influences how well these technologies interact with your skin.

Glide and Comfort

The treatment head moves across the skin for several minutes. Without lubrication, the micro-sculpting pulses and massage vibrations can tug or drag, especially on thin, crepey arms or necks. A serum creates a smooth surface, allowing the device to travel without pulling delicate tissue. This reduces redness, discomfort, and the risk of overworking sensitive areas.

Thermal and Light Conduction

The mild thermal boost aims to improve microcirculation. A hydrated skin surface can absorb and distribute warmth more evenly. Similarly, while red light penetrates regardless of topical products, a clean, moist environment may reduce surface reflection, allowing slightly more light to reach the dermis where collagen synthesis happens. No serum is not a barrier, but a serum can optimize conditions.

Using MyoGlow Without Serum: What to Expect

Some users start without a serum, either to test the device or to avoid extra cost. Here’s what happens.

Pros:

  • No additional product cost
  • Simple, fast routine
  • Compatible with post-treatment application of your own skincare

Cons:

  • More friction and potential skin tugging
  • Reduced thermal massage comfort
  • Possibly slower visible results due to suboptimal contact
  • Not recommended for thin or fragile skin

Verdict: Using MyoGlow serum-free is safe but often less effective and less comfortable. It is acceptable for an occasional session, but daily use without a serum may lead to irritation and underdeliver on the device’s potential.

Choosing the Best Serum for MyoGlow

Not all serums are equal. The right formula enhances the treatment while nourishing the skin.

Key Features to Look For

  • Water-Based and Lightweight: Avoid heavy oils or silicones that block light and leave a greasy residue. Look for hyaluronic acid, glycerin, or aloe-based formulas.
  • Conductivity: Serums with a thin, slippery consistency allow the device to glide without drag.
  • Skin-Beneficial Ingredients: Peptides, niacinamide, and antioxidants support skin repair and complement the collagen-stimulating effect of the light therapy.
  • Absorption Profile: The serum should absorb within minutes and not create a sticky film that impedes movement.

Alternatives to the Official MyoGlow No-Tox Serum

The official No-Tox serum included in many bundles is designed specifically for the device, but similar drugstore serums can serve if you prefer.

  • Hyaluronic Acid Serums: Brands like The Ordinary or Vichy Mineral 89 provide slip and intense hydration.
  • Aloe Vera Gel (Pure): A natural, conductive option with a cooling effect, suitable for sensitive skin.
  • Water-Based Peptide Serums: These support collagen production while offering a smooth glide.

Avoid: oil-based serums, thick creams, or anything with petrolatum, as these can clog pores and interfere with light therapy.

Our Verdict: Serum Matters More Than You Think

While MyoGlow works without serum, the experience and outcome gap is significant. The device’s micro-sculpting and massage elements are designed for a friction-free environment, and a compatible serum can accelerate skin smoothing and firming. The official serum costs little when bundled, and it removes guesswork. If you prefer to shop elsewhere, just stick to simple, water-based options. A few extra dollars for the right glide can be the difference between a comfortable daily habit and a skipped session.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use MyoGlow with just water or a facial mist?
Yes, a light mist of water or thermal spring water can provide temporary slip, but it dries quickly, so you may need to reapply. Serum is more durable and beneficial.
Will any moisturizer work as a MyoGlow serum?
Most moisturizers are too thick and creamy. They can create a greasy barrier that reduces micro-sculpting contact. Use a thin, gel-based serum instead for the best balance.
Is the official serum necessary for results, or is it just an upsell?
It’s not strictly necessary, but it is formulated for conductivity and contains ingredients that support skin renewal. Any water-based, non-greasy serum can substitute effectively.
How much serum should I apply per session?
Just enough to create a thin, even layer that allows the device to glide without catching. A few drops or a small pump is typically sufficient for one treatment area.
